📖 How to Play

⚔️ Combat

This is an auto-battler. Your hero fights automatically. Watch them clash!

📊 Stats

Attack: Base damage per hit (+2 per point)
Attack Speed: Attacks per second (+0.12 per point, max 6.0)
Critical: Double damage chance (+2% per point, max 75%)
Lifesteal: Heal for % of damage dealt (+2% per point, max 40%)
Defense: Reduces incoming damage (+2 per point)
HP: Health points (+10 per point)

🗼 Floors & Progression

Start with 5 stat points. Every 5 floors, gain 5 more points to improve your build.

Boss Floors: Floors 11, 21, 31... grant +3 bonus stat points (8 total: 5 normal + 3 boss bonus).

Enemies get stronger each floor. Adaptive AI counters your strategy!

⚡ Battle Speed

Toggle between 1x, 3x, and 5x speed during combat to accelerate battles!

🔥 Active Abilities

Unlock your first active ability at floor 15, then choose a new one every 15 floors (15, 30, 45...).

Each ability is single-use per combat - use them wisely at critical moments!

You can keep your current ability or swap it for a new one when offered.

💀 Permadeath

One life per run. If you lose, your run ends. Top 3 runs are saved!

🎯 Goal

Climb as high as you can. Build wisely, adapt, and reach the top!

🔄 Relics

Start with 1 relic at the beginning, then gain 1 relic every 10 floors (10, 20, 30...). You can equip up to 3 relics at once. If you already have 3, you can replace one with a new one.

⚡ Relics List

Shield System:

💎 Diamond Shield: Gain a shield equal to 25% max HP at battle start. Shield prevents critical hits and absorbs damage first

🔄 Shield Battery: Shield regenerates 10% of max HP every 5 seconds (if shield is broken)

Offensive Relics:

⚔️ Berserker Rage: +15% Attack, -10% Max HP

💥 Critical Mass: Crits deal 2.5x damage instead of 2x

🎯 First Blood: First hit always crits

🔪 Armor Piercing: Ignore 30% of enemy defense

⚡ Double Strike: 15% chance to attack twice

☠️ Execute: Deal 3x damage to enemies below 20% HP

🩸 Bleed: Attacks apply 5% max HP bleed over 3s

⚔️ Rage Combo: Each consecutive hit increases damage by 5% (max 50%, resets on miss)

🎯 Weak Point: Each attack ignores 2% more of enemy defense (max 60%)

🔥 Spite: Below 30% HP, deal +60% damage

⚔️ Executioner: Deal +50% damage to enemies below 40% HP

🎯 Precision Strike: Attacks cannot miss, but deal -10% damage

Defensive Relics:

🌬️ Second Wind: Heal 25% HP when dropping below 30% (once per battle)

🌵 Thorns: Reflect 20% of damage taken

🛡️ Fortify: +50 flat HP

🏰 Shield Wall: +15 Defense, -15% Attack Speed

💚 Regeneration: Heal 2% max HP per second

⚔️ Last Stand: Survive lethal damage once at 1 HP, gain +50% damage for 5s

🐘 Thick Skin: Take 15% less damage from all sources

🛡️ Battle Hardened: Each hit taken increases defense by 1 (max +20, resets on heal above 80% HP)

💫 Blink: 20% chance to dodge any attack completely

🛡️ Iron Will: Take 10% less damage from critical hits, +20 Defense

⚔️ Retaliate: First 5 hits received, counter-attack immediately for 40% of normal damage

Utility/Hybrid Relics:

🧛 Vampire: +15% Lifesteal (flat)

💨 Haste: +0.5 Attack Speed

📈 Momentum: Deal 1% more damage per second of combat (max 30%)

🩸 Blood Pact: Lose 10% max HP, gain +20% damage and +10% lifesteal

💪 Adrenaline: Below 50% HP, gain +25% attack speed

⚖️ Balanced Stance: +5% to all stats

⚡ Power Spike: Every 5 floors, gain +10% to all stats (permanent)

⚗️ Potion Master: Heal 3% max HP every 3 seconds, but lose 1% max HP permanently each floor

🔄 Recycle: When you dodge or miss, gain +15% attack speed for 3 seconds

👻 Soul Collector: Each floor completed, gain +1 Attack and +5 Max HP permanently

⚡ Frenzy: Each successful hit increases attack speed by 5% (max +50%, resets on taking damage)
